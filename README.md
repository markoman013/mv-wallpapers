<h1 align="center">MV Wallpapers</h1>

A hand-picked collection of 1080p-and-up wallpapers for **Android TV / Google TV**, with a
**Projectivy Launcher** plugin that rotates them as your home-screen background.

Collections: Architecture · Landscapes · Sea · Mountains · Space · Animals · Art,
plus seasonal picks (Spring · Summer · Fall · Winter).

## What you need

- An Android TV or Google TV device.
- [Projectivy Launcher](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.spocky.projengmenu) installed and set as your launcher.

## Install

1. Download the latest APK, **`MV-Wallpapers-Plugin-v….apk`**, from
   **[Releases → latest](https://github.com/markoman013/mv-wallpapers/releases/latest)**.
2. Install it on your TV, using one of these:
   - the **Downloader** app on the TV (enter the link above), or
   - download it on your phone or computer and send it to the TV with a sideloading tool
     (for example *Send Files to TV*, atvTools or ADB).

   Android will ask you to allow installing apps from that source the first time.
3. Open **Projectivy → Settings → Appearance → Wallpaper** and pick **MV Wallpapers** as the source.

The plugin has no icon of its own. You open it from Projectivy's wallpaper settings.

## Using it

- **Collections and Seasons tabs:** highlight a card to see its numbers in the bottom bar.
  **OK** opens a collection. **Hold OK** opens options (select, deselect, hide).
- **Inside a collection:** **OK** shows a photo full screen. **Hold OK** opens options.
- **Select** button: cycles **all → none → custom**. Custom remembers your own picks.
- **Show** filters by selected or not selected. **Sort** orders by newest or by name.
- **Settings tab:**
  - wallpapers per hour
  - seasonal rule (seasonal photos only while their season is selected, or always)
  - blur on menus
  - reshuffle
  - check for updates
- **Updates:** the plugin tells you when a new version is out and installs it for you.

Every wallpaper is shown once in random order before any repeats. New photos join automatically.

> **Move, Edit tags and Delete** change the collection on GitHub, so they need the
> owner's GitHub token. As a guest you can ignore them. Everything else works without one.
